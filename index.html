CULTURAL HERITAGE × AUGMENTED REALITY

🏺 Heritage AR Guide

Explore cultural heritage artifacts through interactive browser-based Augmented Reality.

How the experience works

📷 Marker-Based AR

Point your camera at the provided Moonstone image to reveal a 3D cultural heritage artifact.

🌐 Markerless AR

Detect a real-world surface and place, rotate, scale, change, or remove a virtual artifact.

Before you start

  1. Use a compatible mobile browser.
  2. Allow camera access when requested.
  3. For Marker AR, scan the Moonstone image.
  4. For Markerless AR, move the phone slowly until a suitable surface is detected.

Technologies Used

Three.js · WebXR · A-Frame · MindAR · GLTF/GLB
